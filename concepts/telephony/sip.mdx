Use this guide to connect your SBC/PBX to Inquira for inbound and outbound calling.

## Inbound SIP Configuration

Send incoming calls from your network to Inquira’s SIP edge.

- **Signaling**
  - Protocol: SIP
  - Transports & ports:
    - UDP 5060
    - TCP 5060
    - TLS 5061 (SIPS)
  - Ensure your SBC/PBX can reach our hosts on the relevant ports (egress from you → ingress to us).
- **Media**
  - Codecs: G.722, G.711 (PCMU/PCMA)
  - Encryption: SRTP supported
- **Source IP whitelisting (required)**
  - We restrict ingress by source IP. Provide the public IP address(es) your SBC/PBX uses to originate calls so we can whitelist them. Calls from non-whitelisted IPs are rejected.
- **SIP edge hosts**
  - Staging: `sip-staging-1.inquira.app`, `sip-staging-2.inquira.app`, `sip-staging-3.inquira.app`
  - Production: `sip1.inquira.app`, `sip2.inquira.app`, `sip3.inquira.app`
  - Recommendation: configure all three hosts for round-robin and failover.
- **Call routing**
  - To: route inbound calls to the selected Inquira host over your chosen transport.
  - From: present CLI/ANI in E.164.
  - Early media: supported.
  - Failover recommendation: configure a failsafe destination number on your SBC/PBX so if Inquira does not respond within your timeout threshold, calls automatically forward to a live line.
- **TLS/SRTP notes (if using encrypted signaling/media)**
  - SIP over TLS: connect to 5061 using `tls` transport.
  - Media: offer/answer with SRTP for encrypted RTP.
  - Certificates: use standard public CAs and ensure your SBC trusts common roots.
- **What we need from you**
  - Source IPs for whitelisting (all egress addresses you might use).
  - Environment you’ll connect to (staging or production).
  - Transport you intend to use (UDP/TCP/TLS).
  - Codec list you will offer (must include G.722 and/or G.711).
- **Optional SBC/PBX hints**
  - Enable keepalives/OPTIONS if supported.
  - Set reasonable SIP and media timeouts and enable failover across our host list.
  - Prefer G.722 when wideband is available; fall back to G.711.
  - Test call-forwarding/failover routing periodically to confirm resiliency.

If you need test windows or a specific routing/CNAM policy, share details along with your source IPs and we’ll complete the whitelist and confirm readiness.

## Outbound SIP Configuration

- We support URL- or auth-based outbound trunks; provide the SIP URI for your trunk plus credentials if required.
- Transports: UDP, TCP, TLS.
- Media: SRTP supported; codecs G.722 and G.711 (PCMU/PCMA).
- Share your desired transport, authentication details, and any CNAM/routing requirements so we can validate and confirm readiness.
